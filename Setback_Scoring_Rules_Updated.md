# 🧮 Setback Scoring System – In Depth

## 🎯 Goal:
- Be the first team to reach **21 points**
- Points are earned **only through captured cards**
- Points are awarded **at the end of each round**, after all 6 tricks have been played

---

## 🃏 Total Available Points per Round: 6

| Point Type   | Description                                                                 |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **High**     | Team that captures the **highest-ranking Trump card**                      |
| **Low**      | Team that captures the **lowest-ranking Trump card**                       |
| **Jack**     | Team that captures the **Jack of the Trump suit**                          |
| **Off-Jack** | Team that captures the **Off-Jack** (Jack of same color as Trump suit)     |
| **Joker**    | Team that captures the **Joker**, if played                                 |
| **Game**     | Team with the **highest total point value** from captured cards            |

---

## 🎖️ Point Breakdown

### High
- Goes to the team that captures the **highest-ranking Trump card**

### Low
- Goes to the team that captures the **lowest-ranking Trump card**

### Jack of Trump
- Goes to the team that captures the **Jack of the declared Trump suit**

### Off-Jack
- Goes to the team that captures the **Jack of the same color, different suit** than the Trump suit

### Joker
- Goes to the team that captures the **Joker**, if it is played

### Game
- Goes to the team with the **highest card value total** from all captured tricks

| Card        | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| 10          | 10    |
| Ace         | 4     |
| King        | 3     |
| Queen       | 2     |
| Jack        | 1     |
| All others  | 0     |

If tied, **no Game point is awarded**.

---

## 📝 Additional Scoring Rules

### 🔹 Bidding Risk
- The team that won the bid must **meet or exceed** their bid to earn points.
- If they **fail**, they **lose the full bid amount**, regardless of how many points they actually earned.

### 🔹 Non-Bidding Team Still Scores
- The **non-bidding team keeps any points they earn** from captured cards.
- This applies even if the bidding team fails and loses points.

> ✅ This means **both teams can earn or lose points independently** in the same round.

### 🔹 6 and Out
- If a team bids “6 and Out” and earns **all 6 points**, they win instantly.
- If they **fail to earn all 6**, they lose instantly.

### 🔹 Negative Scores Allowed
- Teams can have **negative total scores** due to failed bids.

---

## ✅ End-of-Round Summary
1. Determine who won each of the 6 points.
2. Tally points for both teams.
3. Apply bidding rules:
   - If bidding team met bid → they keep earned points.
   - If not → they lose the full bid amount.
4. Non-bidding team keeps whatever they earned.
5. Check for game end:
   - First to 21 points
   - Or 6 and Out success/failure
